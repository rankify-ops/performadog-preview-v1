Nutritional integrity

Every ingredient is chosen for biological value — not for the label.

Performadog started as a boutique food for the dogs Kris bred and trained — Dobermanns, German Shepherds, police and competition dogs. It was tested and measured on real working animals for years before it was ever sold to the public.

Every ingredient is researched for purity and potency so nutrients arrive at a cellular level. Our factory, suppliers and processes are audited. Our fresh regional ingredients genuinely meet the AAFCO definition of “fresh”.
