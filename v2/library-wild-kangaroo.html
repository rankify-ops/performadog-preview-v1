Wild Kangaroo

Why wild kangaroo meat suits dogs and cats with food intolerances: a novel, lean protein high in CLA.

2019 · 2 min read

Preview note: fix before launch

Wild Kangaroo Meat

Itching, scratching, patches of hair missing, digestive upset, recurrent ear infections, oily fur, chewing on paws..what do all of these have in common?  They may all be signs of a food allergy or food intolerance.

Dogs and cats who have not eaten Kangaroo meats before often do well on them because their systems have not developed an intolerance or an allergy to them.

Kangaroo is an exceptional meat and worth the time to look into, especially if you have a dog that hasn’t been able to thrive on any other proteins.  One of the best things about kangaroo meat is the presence of conjugated linoleic acids, also known as CLAs.

Conjugated linoleic acid is a naturally occurring free fatty acid. It is a potent antioxidant, which may improve lipid profiles and enhance total health.  CLA has been credited with naturally occurring free fatty acid found in milk, cheese, beef, and lamb. It is a potent antioxidant, which may improve lipid profiles and enhance total health, improving cholesterol, aiding weight loss, improving the immune system and even aiding the body in the fight against cancer. Wild kangaroo meat has the highest naturally occurring CLAs. Dog with chronic troubles can benefit from all this extra immune support. CLAs also help reduce inflammation in the body, which can significantly reduce inflammatory allergic reactions like colitis, dry, itchy skin and many other conditions.

Kangaroo meat is also high in protein but low in fat.  Cats with digestive troubles may see improvement switching to this meat since the low-fat meat is easy to digest.

Kangaroo, as a meat ingredient in cat food, has many advantages that make it an ideal source of protein.  Here are some key benefits in kangaroo:

  • it is naturally the leanest red meat protein so is an ideal choice for pets suffering from conditions requiring low-fat diets (such as pancreatitis)
  • the low levels of fat that are present are unsaturated fats (the right kind!)
  • kangaroo is among the best zinc and iron levels of any meat
  • all kangaroos are wild; there is no farming in existence, so they live humane, free lives in a natural habitat.
