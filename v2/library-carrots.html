Carrots' beta-carotene, fibre and antioxidants, and their benefits for dogs.

Carrots

The carrot (Daucus carota) is a root vegetable that is often claimed to be one of the perfect health foods. It is crunchy, tasty and highly nutritious. Carrots are a particularly good source of beta-carotene, fibre, vitamin K, potassium and antioxidants.

What Are The Benefits of Carrots?

Carrots contain vitamin A, antioxidants, and other nutrients. Evidence suggests that eating more antioxidant-rich fruits and vegetables, such as carrots, can help reduce the risks of cancer and cardiovascular disease. Carrots are also rich in vitamins, minerals, and fibre.

  • Vitamin A: Healthy dog vitamin.
  • Regularity: Curing canine constipation and benefits of fibre.
  • Low in fat and calories.
  • Dog dental health.
  • Beta-Carotene: Improve your dog’s coat colour and eyesight.
