What it shows, and what it doesn't

One dog's documented results. Not a promise for every dog.

This is a single dog, not a controlled trial. Diet, training, age and health all affect bloodwork, so we can't say the food alone caused these changes. We publish the full reports so you and your vet can read them yourselves.
