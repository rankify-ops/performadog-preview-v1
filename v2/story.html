Our story

The food a breeder built for his own dogs.

Kris Kotsopoulos has trained working dogs since 1980 and bred them since 1982. Performadog came out of one lesson learned the hard way: you can't train or breed your way past poor nutrition.

01

A dog let him down when it counted

Kris had trained the dog well. In a real situation it still didn't have the courage or robustness the job needed. Training alone wasn't enough, and that sent him into genetics and breeding in 1982.

02

Two pillars make a performance dog

Years of breeding taught him that selection and nutrition carry a performance animal. If we ask dogs to work like athletes, we have to feed them like athletes.

03

No food met the standard

Pro Plan sponsored his kennel for six years. When production moved, his dogs started losing condition, and nothing else on the shelf did what he needed.

04

So he built it

With animal nutritionist Bill Wiadrowski, Kris developed the food he wanted for his own dogs and fed it to his kennel from 2015. It worked, the trainers and breeders around him asked for it, and Performadog became available to everyone.

“If we are asking dogs to perform at a high level, we are essentially asking them to function like athletes.”Kris Kotsopoulos · confirm wording

Detector Dogs Australia

Dogs that work where it matters

Kris co-founded Detector Dogs Australia in 2000. Its teams search venues like the MCG and Marvel Stadium, the Port of Melbourne, hospitals and major events. Kris has been interviewed by SBS and Channel 9 about the work.

File 01 · 45 years with working dogs

From one Dobermann to Detector Dogs Australia

1980

First Dobermann

At 16, after the family home was broken into, Kris bought his first Dobermann and started training dogs.

1982

Von Forell begins

A dog let him down in a real situation. Training wasn't enough, so Kris turned to genetics and started breeding under the Von Forell name.

1984

1984

Working dogs for hire

Kris sets up a dog security company in Melbourne, putting his dogs to work every day.

1993

1993

Raising the standard

Joins the National Dog Trainers Federation and starts a frozen semen bank to protect the best bloodlines.

2000

Detector Dogs Australia

Co-founds Detector Dogs Australia. Its teams now search venues like the MCG and the Port of Melbourne.

2003

National champion

Von Forell Vilco wins the National Australian Schutzhund Championship. Kris is made a Life Member of the NDTF.

2015

Performadog in the kennel

After his sponsor's food changed and his dogs lost condition, Kris starts trialling his own formula on the Von Forell dogs.

2018

2018

Bill Wiadrowski joins

Animal nutritionist Bill Wiadrowski, formulating diets since 1986, joins to refine the range.
