Yeast Extract

How Performadog's yeast extract is made and how its MOS and beta-glucans support gut health and immunity.

2019 · 1 min read

Yeast Extract

To produce yeast extract, the contents of the yeast cell are broken down by enzymes, and the cell wall is collected. But not all yeast cells were created equal, and we only use the cell wall material from very specific strains of yeast. These strains are selected for their mannan oligosaccharide (MOS) and beta-glucan content. More than 50% of the yeast material used by Performadog consists of these two vital nutrients.

What Are The Benefits of Yeast Cell Wall Material For Dogs?

The mannan oligosaccharide (MOS) binds specific pathogens by a process called agglutination.  Put simply, agglutination is the clumping of pathogens into one large complex, thus making it simpler and easier for the immune system to eliminate the clump in one action rather than having to eliminate each pathogen individually. MOS is particularly effective at clumping type 1 fimbriae pathogens, various strains of Salmonella and E.coli bacteria.

B-glucans are a vital source of phagocytic cells which are capable of killing pathogens, by attracting and engulfing the bacteria. After completing the destruction of the pathogen the phagocyte moves the remaining material back to the surface of the cell to educate the immune system as to the danger presented by the pathogen. This is what is called the “immune response” within the body, and B-glucans play a dominant role in educating and stimulating the animal’s immune system.
