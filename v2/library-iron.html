Why Does The Body Need Iron?

Iron is an essential mineral. “The primary reason is that it helps to transport oxygen throughout the body.

Iron is an essential component of haemoglobin, the substance in red blood cells that carries oxygen from the lungs to transport it throughout the body. Haemoglobin represents about two-thirds of the body’s iron. If the body doesn’t have enough iron, it can’t make enough healthy oxygen-carrying red blood cells. A lack of red blood cells is called iron deficiency anaemia.

Without healthy red blood cells, the body can’t get enough oxygen. If your dog is not getting sufficient oxygen in the body, it will begin to become fatigued. That exhaustion can affect everything from its brain function to its immune system’s ability to fight off infections. If your breeding females become pregnant and have a severe iron deficiency, it may increase your puppies risk of being born too early, or smaller than normal.

Iron is also necessary to maintain healthy cells, skin, hair, and nails.
