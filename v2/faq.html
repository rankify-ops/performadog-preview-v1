FAQ

Questions

The ones we get asked most. Anything else, call Kris on 0418 348 804.

The food

Is it only for working dogs?

No. It was built to working-dog standards and plenty of active family dogs eat it. The feeding guide sets the right food and amount for your dog.

Which food should I choose?

Turkey Grain Free for dogs that work, train or compete. Ocean Fish for itchy skin, allergies or a sensitive stomach. Puppy Lamb & Fish while they grow.

Is it complete and balanced?

Yes — formulated to meet Australian and AAFCO nutrient standards. Exact statement to confirm

Is there chicken in it?

The dog range has no chicken. Turkey Grain Free contains poultry oil. iPurr Kangaroo was made for cats that react to chicken.

Who makes it?

It is made in Australia under HACCP and GMP. Manufacturer details Kris is happy to show

Feeding

How much do I feed?

Start from the bag table, or use the feeding guide. Most owners feed less by weight than their last food.

How do I switch over?

Over about 10 days: 25%, then half, then 75%, then all Performadog.

Can I add raw meat or bones?

Many of our handlers do. Bill: one line of guidance

Ordering & delivery

How much is delivery?

Metro orders over $150 ship free. Regional freight is shown before you pay.

How fast is it?

Dispatched within 24 hours. Metro usually 2–4 business days, regional 5–7.

How do subscriptions work?

Pick the food and how often. We send it before you run out. Skip, pause or cancel online.

What if my dog won't eat it?

Return the bag at least 80% full with your invoice for a refund.
